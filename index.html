Health Coach

Health Coach is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single person, its developer. It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission it reads their own fitness and sleep data from the Google Health API and their own Google Calendar (read-only), keeps a copy on their own computer, and produces a daily summary, charts and a morning briefing email sent only to themselves. A personal AI coach uses this data to comment on sleep and recovery and suggest suitable exercise.

No other users, accounts or data are involved.
