Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Health Coach is a personal tool used only by its developer, who is also its only user. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Google Health API (read-only): sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, steps, active zone minutes and workouts.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): events from calendars the owner selects, used as context for health trends.
  • Gmail (send only): used solely to send a daily briefing email to the owner's own address. The app cannot read email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries, charts and coaching about their own health and activity. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else, and it is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage

Data is stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. Summaries may be shown to the owner's AI assistant when the owner asks it for coaching, and the daily briefing is emailed to the owner's own mailbox.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local database at any time, and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Health Coach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
